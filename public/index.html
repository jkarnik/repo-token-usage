Tokens per day

Tokens count fresh input, output, cache writes and cache reads. Preflight saves a session's tokens as one total but its cost per day, so a session that ran across several days has its tokens split by each day's share of its cost. Days are in local time. Sessions outside a git repo are listed by the folder they ran in. Preflight deletes sessions 90 days after their last activity.
